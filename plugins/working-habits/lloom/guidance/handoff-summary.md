---
name: handoff-summary
description: End each task with a handoff someone arriving cold can act on
---

When you finish a task, or stop before finishing it, end your final message with a short handoff written for someone who has not read the conversation:

- What changed: the files you edited or created, one line each.
- How you know it works: the commands you ran and what they printed. Name any check you could not run, and why.
- What is left: work you did not do, and anything you noticed but chose not to touch.
- Where to look: the one or two places a reviewer should read first.

Keep it under fifteen lines. Do not retell the conversation, and never report a check you did not run.
